After WWII, the Nuremberg Trials put Nazi leaders before an international court. The trials showed that leaders could be judged for war crimes and crimes against humanity.

NOW

Today, the United Nations gives countries a place to debate conflicts before they become world wars. It does not stop every crisis, but it reflects the lesson the world took from WWII: ignoring aggression and genocide can become deadly.
